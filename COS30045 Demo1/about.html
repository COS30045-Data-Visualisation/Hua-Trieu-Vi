About EnergyWatch AU

Promoting energy literacy and practical household savings across Australia through transparent data.

Our Mission

EnergyWatch AU is dedicated to guiding consumers toward smarter appliance choices. We analyze energy consumption data from official registration databases to highlight practical ways households can lower power bills without compromising on entertainment quality or comfort.

With electricity tariffs rising across Australian states, appliance selection plays a pivotal role in long-term budget sustainability.

Data & Methodology

Our television efficiency report is driven by real-world data from official Australian registration databases (tv_2026_02_15.csv):

  • Sample Size: 4,508 individual TV model registrations evaluated.
  • Size Categorization (KNIME Standard Rules):
    • Small: ≤ 43 inches (1,111 models | Avg: 158.11 kWh/year)
    • Medium: 44 – 65 inches (2,059 models | Avg: 404.68 kWh/year)
    • Large: ≥ 66 inches (1,338 models | Avg: 748.88 kWh/year)
  • Key Metrics Tracked: Labelled Annual Energy Consumption (kWh/year), Screen Size (inch).

Academic Background & Tools

This project was developed for COS30045 Data Visualisation at Swinburne University of Technology. Built using:

  • HTML5 & CSS3 for responsive, accessible web structure and custom layout styling
  • KNIME Analytics Platform for data cleaning, aggregation, and rule-based categorization
  • Python (Pandas & Matplotlib) for dataset validation and chart generation
  • Git & GitHub for version management and web hosting
